June 4, 2023

I shopped at Daiso for the first time. I bought a bowl, some chips, and Demon Slayer curry. I recall accidentally scanning one of the items twice like a dumb gaijin and a worker needing to assist me.

I obtained an IC card which I would use for the remainder of my time in Japan. I actually still have it to this day.

This was the day of the first class excursion. The excursion was to Osaka Castle. We traveled to Osaka Castle as one big group.

We didn't actually go inside of the castle. Once we reached the outside, the professor explained that we could basically do whatever we wanted in Osaka.

I decided to stick with the professor (I mean, he's the expert on the area so I trusted he knew the cool spots to go). Me, the prof, and two other students headed for Osaka Umeda.

We went inside of this building where I saw my first Jump store.

We ended up eating at an Omurice place. I ordered Omurice in mushroom/spinach soup. Another student joined us while we were sat down, making it five of us. I did not have location tagging on my photos at the time, but using photos/videos before/after the event + my menu item note + my memory, I have high confidence that the place we ate at was Pomme's LINKS Umeda, 8F.

After eating, we went to an electronics store, an anime store, and a couple arcades.

I would rate this excursion: D-
Reason: I would rate the day as an A for being a fun time but the excursion itself (Osaka Castle) was basically nonexistent because we didn't go inside.

June 9, 2023

This was the day of the second class excursion. The excursion was to Kiyomizu Temple. It was cool.

After finishing with the temple, I ate a cinnamon custard thing from one of the vendors nearby.

I decided to walk all the way from Kiyomizu back to the area of Kyoto where we were staying. I recall wandering onto one of those shrine type areas at one point during my walk and I got "Dame"'d off the premises. I think I must have wandered past a payment point to visit the shrine or something idk. I continued my walk over to this shopping center which included a Disney store and a massive Ed-On which I went inside. After exploring these shops, I ate a strange mayo/corn filled pastry from a vending machine.

I then went back to Round1, where I wasted about $50 on claw machines. All I had to show for it were some chocolate cookies. I played some MaiMai as well.

I ate at a sandwich shop.

I would rate this excursion: C
Reason: It was cool and all but it's not a place I would visit if it wasn't so famous.

June 16, 2023

This was the day of the third class excursion. The excursion was to Fushimi Inari Shrine. This was one of my favorites. I love walking/hiking so the challenge to reach the top was awesome. It also had the iconic red Japanese gates.

According to my notes, I found another huge mall. However, I am not sure which mall I was referring to. After checking out the mall, I went to Movix to watch Spider-man Across the Spiderverse (the first day it released in Japan!). I watched the movie with Japanese subs, meaning that I could just listen to the dialogue in English. It was awesome, actually my favorite movie of the year. I also ordered a medium popcorn.

After the movie, I ate at a stand-up ramen shop. I ordered curry ramen and it was extremely good. Probably my favorite ramen of the entire trip.

I returned to my room at Stay Sakura and my roommate was gone (he had gone to Osaka).

I would rate this excursion: A+
Reason: I like hiking and it felt very Japanese.

June 23, 2023

This was the day of the fourth class excursion. The excursion was to multiple sake refineries in Kobe. We went to five in total. We got some cool tours and lots of sake to taste test. This was actually my first time ever tasting alcohol. I'm dead serious. I was 21 at the time. I didn't drink on my 21st birthday and had never even taken a sip as a kid. Even to this day (I'm 24), the only time I have ever drank alcohol is during travel excursions that involve taste testing.

Anyways, maybe because I wasn't used to the taste of alcohol, but every single sake I tried was gross af. 

The professor recommended beforehand that we check out the famous "Kobe beef." I went with a group of 5+ other people to Steakland to try Kobe beef. It was pretty good but also pretty expensive.

Prior to this day, one of the other students posted on Teams that they wanted to take a trip to Hiroshima. I reached out to go with them (see Rule 1). After we ate at steakland, me and the others who were going to Hiroshima regrouped at Kobe Chinatown. There were four of us.

We rode the Shinkansen (bullet train) to Hiroshima. This was my first time riding the Shinkansen. I was extremely lucky when buying a ticket, I had essentially the exact amount of cash in my pocket for it. I had no idea how expensive it was beforehand. It lowkey would have been awkward for my group if I was short lol. 

The four of us arrived at an Airbnb in Hiroshima.

I would rate this excursion: A
Reason: Cool experience. I don't like sake.
